# NoBrainer design notes

## Structure (2026-10-25)

NoBrainer is BetterBrainer plus the Skitarius servo-skull command, Symbols reroll (with exact lookahead from the terminal's seed), Search transition skip (user decision, 2026-10-25) and the Drill seed-predicted opening (user decision, 2026-10-28). BetterBrainer itself is 1.0.4 plus the Frequency pipeline; the shared solver design is in `mods/active/BetterBrainer/DESIGN.md`. This file covers what differs: the reopen controller, the two features that use it, and the Drill opening.

- `NoBrainer.lua`: BetterBrainer's core (`BetterBrainer.lua`) plus the reopen controller, the client seed counter (`ctx.initial_seed`, used by Symbols and Drill) and the entry-frame lookup of the minigame being entered. It also has the ModID, path and data-file names changed, a one-time migration of 3.1-era speed settings (`_speed_schema_v2`), and the servo skull module, which `mod.update` calls every frame while the mod is enabled.
- `frequency.lua`, `scan.lua`, `balance.lua`: identical copies of BetterBrainer's modules. Keep them identical: change BetterBrainer first, then copy. The Balance centre hold (below) was built here first by user decision and copied to BetterBrainer after its in-game checks (2026-10-28).
- `drill.lua`: BetterBrainer's module plus the seed-predicted opening and `completed` (the fast exit's check for this session's receipts). A change to the shared Drill solver is made in both mods by hand.
- `symbols.lua`, `search.lua`: BetterBrainer's modules plus Symbols reroll and Search transition skip. BetterBrainer's versions are the 1.0.4 files, so a change to those solvers is made in both mods.
- `servo_skull.lua`: NoBrainer's servo-skull command as a core module (`return function(ctx) ... end` with `update(dt, t)`, `reset`, `settings_changed`). It reads `ctx.settings`; its logic is unchanged from 3.2.0.
- `NoBrainer_data.lua`, `NoBrainer_localization.lua`: BetterBrainer's options and strings plus `decode_reroll_limit`, `enable_expedition_reopen` and the servo skull group, with NoBrainer's name and description.

Never enable NoBrainer and BetterBrainer together: both automate the same input.

## Balance centre hold (`balance.lua`)
- Exactly centred is impossible. The server owns the position. Every 2.5 s while progressing it adds a 0.6 velocity kick in one of eight integer-radian directions, from its own `math.random(0, math.pi * 2)` (LuaJIT floors the bounds), and input can only accelerate it at 4.2 per axis (`minigame_balance.lua:89-187`, `minigame_settings.lua:8-13`). Online the solver sees a kick half a round trip late, and its reply runs half a round trip plus a tick later. The outward push (1.08 at the centre) points along the position, so the centre is a discontinuity a lagged hold wobbles around.
- The 1.0.4 controller scaled a critically damped command by 0.35, so it also only cancelled 35 % of the push. It settled about 0.13 radii off centre and swung out to about 0.3.
- Now: the push is cancelled in full, and the steering follows a velocity target toward the centre (rate 3 per second near it, a 3/s² braking curve further out, gain 8). A saturated input keeps its direction. Samples blend 0.8 of the position residual and 0.5 of the velocity residual (was 0.65 and 0.20), so a kick is learned within a tick or two. The edge-braking fallback is unchanged.
- Offline (`tests/balance_spec.lua`): RMS distance 0.023 radii as host, 0.037 at 30 ms, 0.053 at 80 ms and 0.080 at 150 ms (1.0.4: 0.18-0.21). Worst kick peaks 0.07, 0.13, 0.18 and 0.25 (1.0.4: 0.29-0.35). The hold without kicks is 0.002 as host and 0.02-0.03 online. No case leaves the circle, including input running two ticks later than assumed and 0.004-step position samples. In game it matched these rows (Runtime status, 2026-10-28).

## Reopen controller (`NoBrainer.lua`)
- Why: a decoder terminal's `decoding` interaction takes 0 s (`interaction_templates.lua:36-43`) and is allowed again 0.1 s after the last success while the minigame is inactive (`decoder_device_extension.lua:292-305`). Every entry runs `setup_game` on the server before that frame's input (`player_character_state_minigame.lua:138-148,376-402`).
  - Search keeps its board and stage, recentres the cursor and is in gameplay at once (`minigame_decode_search.lua:68-86`; `minigame_base.lua:34-36,82-92`). Reopening therefore replaces the 0.25 s transition (`minigame_decode_search.lua:163-165`) with a few frames.
  - Symbols builds a new board on every setup (`minigame_decode_symbols.lua:93-144`).
  - Drill cannot use this, because its setup resets the stage to 1 (`minigame_drill.lua:71-94`).
- How: a solver calls `ctx.reopen_ready(mg, t)`, then `ctx.request_reopen(mg)`. The controller then:
  1. Injects the ordinary ephemeral cancel (`action_two_pressed`, `player_character_state_minigame.lua:206,307-309`).
  2. On the local `on_exit`, suspends the solver instead of resetting it.
  3. Injects `interact_pressed` (`base_interaction.lua:57-59`) once the interactor would pick the terminal (`InteractorExtension._find_interaction_object`, `interactor_extension.lua:567-588`), `can_interact(terminal, "decoding")` holds, and 0.15 s have passed since the session started. That is 0.05 s of margin over the native gate.
  4. When the new session of the same minigame starts, resumes the solver (`module.resume`). Only after its own interact: before it, the same minigame is the old session replayed by a client correction (`on_enter_server_corrected_state` initializes the terminal again, `player_character_state_minigame.lua:127-148`), and is ignored. Seen in game 2026-10-25: "resumed 0.020s after the request" with no interact, then "session ended 0.000s after the resume, reopening switched off".
  While the reopen is in flight every routed action is neutral, so a held fire or jump key cannot act in the walking state.
- Precondition at request: the terminal is a `decoding` interactee with a decoder device, and the interactor already finds it as chosen or focus target. During the minigame it is only a focus target, because `interaction_allowed` is false while the minigame is active. A focus target the player cannot interact with also passes when nothing is chosen: an expedition terminal sits on a locked chest, which takes the focus but whose block text fails `can_interact` (`chest_extension.lua:21-33`, `interactee_extension.lua:137-177`). Seen in game 2026-10-25 with dt-cli: inside the terminal focus = the chest, so no stage was skipped; after a cancel, without moving, the interactor chose the terminal and could interact. Re-entry still waits until the terminal itself is chosen.
- Timing: the client's interactor runs before the character state machine in a fixed frame (`extension_system_configuration.lua:361,397`), and the interaction completes in the frame it starts (`interactor_extension.lua:325-397`). The sequence is: cancel frame, interact frame (the CSM enters minigame in the same frame), setup frame (that frame's input already counts). The server runs the same frames in order, so the client does not wait for any receipt.
- Failure: no new session within 1 s of the request, or a session lost within 1 s of a resume that does not come back within 1 s (rollback after a server rejection), switches reopening off until the next mission. A lost session usually comes back: seen in game 2026-10-25 (expedition Search), a correction 0.1 s after the resume took the state away, the server had taken the entry a frame later, and the resimulated state entered the same minigame again. The controller keeps the solver while the session is lost and attaches it again unchanged when the same minigame returns (same server session, so no `resume`); before this, reopening switched off and Search resynchronized for 1.6 s. "Lost" means without on_exit: a server correction swaps the state machine's state directly (`character_state_machine.lua:68-81`), while the player's own cancel, dodge or look-away exits through on_exit (`:133-148`) and keeps reopening on (2026-10-25: a manual cancel 0.69 s after a resume had switched it off in game, so later manual entries got no rerolls). A solver still in its old session gets `reopen_failed()`; a suspended one is reset.
- Side effects: the owner also receives the server's `rpc_minigame_sync_stop` for the old session after the new local start (`minigame_base.lua:172-181`). Client stop clears `_player_session_id` and `_action_held` only. The missing-owner path of `ctx.session_valid` already covers that, and every solver releases before pressing again. Teammates see the auspex close and reopen.

## Search transition skip (`search.lua`)
- At speed 5, with `enable_expedition_reopen`, a stage-advancing press below the last stage requests a reopen. Its cursor was confirmed as usual.
- On resume the solver plans the next stage from the centre at once. Until the stage receipt it uses the predicted stage, and it treats the displayed transition state as gameplay. The displayed cursor is ignored until the recentre receipt (`recentred[mg]`, set by the existing `set_cursor_position` hook), with the usual ack timeout.
- Presses still need the receipt stage, a confirmed cursor and the server input-state proof, so a prediction can never cause a wrong press.
- If the press is proven lost (the server ran later timely input and the stage did not advance), it plans the old stage again.
- Recentring also shortens the next path: 4.09 instead of 5.43 steps per board, and no 3-4 step paths (scratch simulation 2026-10-14).

## Symbols reroll (`symbols.lua`)
- At the first moment a fresh board can be pressed (stage 1, complete receipts), the solver computes its own time to the last press. The model is the press rule of `input()`: hit window less 30 ms, frame grid, two frames between presses, a third press waits for the first receipt.
- It rerolls while that time exceeds the reopen delay plus the optimal value of a fresh board with one reroll fewer, plus 0.15 s. The fresh-board value is a finite-horizon optimal stopping value over all 1512 equally likely target sequences, at the measured decision phase and receipt delay.
- The reopen delay starts at ping + 0.15 s and is then measured. The cap is `decode_reroll_limit` per hack.
- The scratch model with ping 0.06-0.2 s gives a mean board of 5.1 s, 4.0-4.2 s at cap 4 (1.5 rerolls on average) and 3.8-4.0 s at cap 8.
- Exact lookahead (from Robert's Smart Seed Reroll): every board a terminal deals follows from its seed (`minigame_decode_symbols.lua:103-140`, `table.lua:781-797`). When that seed is known, the solver deals the next `limit - rerolls` boards itself and values them backwards with the same keep-or-reroll rule, instead of the fresh-board value. Debug lines say `(seed)` or `(average)`.
  - Host: the minigame's `_seed` after setup is the next board's seed.
  - Client: no seed is synced. The server hands extension n `level_seed + n` (`minigame_system.lua:30-71`); safe hooks on `MinigameSystem.init` and `on_add_extension` count the client's own registrations from the `level_seed` in its init data (`gameplay_init_step_extensions.lua:133-136`). Level units register in `Level.units` order on both peers. The solver deals up to 256 boards from the terminal's first seed until one matches the received symbols and targets, so earlier openings by anyone are covered.
  - Unknown seed (no match, hooks registered mid-mission, minigame units spawned at runtime, hot reload): the average board decides, as before.

## Drill seed-predicted opening (`drill.lua`, `NoBrainer.lua`)
- Why: without it the first move waits for the setup receipts (`fresh`), about one receipt delay plus input lag after the entry. The entry frame is sampled before the state initializes the minigame, and the server runs that frame's input right after its own setup (`player_character_state_minigame.lua:138-148,376-402`), so a move planned on the right board selects on the setup frame. The server keeps the stage-1 board while targets remain and otherwise generates one from the minigame's seed (`minigame_drill.lua:71-94,125-166`).
- How: on the entry frame the core resolves the minigame from the state's `minigame_character_state` component, as the state's own initialization does, and asks `drill.entry_input`. At speed 5, with planned aim trusted and seeded openings on, Drill predicts the board and sends the planned aim from the origin when it has a robust winner (`aim`, the same planner as every move):
  - Kept board: the client's own targets (`source kept`).
  - New board: `MinigameDrill.generate_targets` on a scratch table from the client's `_seed`, which the client's last received board left equal to the server's (`seed`), or before any board from the terminal's first seed, `ctx.initial_seed` (`first seed`).
  - On the session start the solver adopts the move as its pending first move. Once the setup receipts arrive it compares the received stage-1 board with the prediction.
- Re-entry: the server's repeat timer survives sessions (`minigame_drill.lua:274`), so no opening within 0.3 s of the terminal's last stop. That is a local stop, or another player's stop received; the server's later argumentless echo of our own stop is ignored.
- Failure: a different board switches seeded openings off for the mission. The move then ran on the received board from the origin, so the solver expects what the native rule selects there, and planned aim and holding stay on. Presses still need this session's receipts, so a wrong prediction costs at most a repeat delay, never a wrong press. A lost entry frame takes the ordinary lost-move path (2.5 s).
- Fast exit: a re-entered terminal shows its last session's completion until this session's receipts arrive, so the Drill fast exit requires them (`drill.completed`).
- Offline (`tests/reopen_spec.lua`, 2026-10-28): kept board 39 of 40 opened on the entry frame; mean solve from the interact 3.045 s against 3.143 s without the opening at 30 ms receipts, 3.224 s against 3.458 s at 165 ms. A re-hack after a decoder interrupt opens from the client's seed with no early exit; this case stalled before the fast-exit fix. Also covered: the first board from a rebuilt seed, a wrong board (switch-off, no mistake, holds continue, no later opening), a quick re-entry (no opening), W held through the entry, and a local server.
